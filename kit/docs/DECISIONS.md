# Decisions

One entry per real choice, newest at the bottom. This is the project's memory: it stops a
later session from undoing something on purpose that was done for a reason.

Log a decision when a later session might undo it without knowing why: architecture choices,
outside libraries (write the exact name in backticks, like `name-of-library`; the check looks for it),
tradeoffs, things tried and dropped, and anything the owner decided. Skip routine naming and
small layout calls. **One or two sentences per entry**; it is read every session. When it passes
about 40 entries, replaced or outdated ones move to `docs/decisions-archive.md`.

**owner** decisions: ask the owner before undoing. **representative** decisions: may be
revised for a good reason, with a new entry saying what changed and why.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD (owner|representative): **What was decided.** Why.`
